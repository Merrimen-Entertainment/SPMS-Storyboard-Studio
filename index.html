Quick start — 5 steps

  1. Type a Project Title (first box, Section 1).
  2. Fill a few boxes — or open any violet — suggest — menu to auto-fill them.
  3. Go to Section 5 and add your shots. Tag each with its Reference Image.
  4. Look at the top: the big prompt is already writing itself as you type.
  5. Press Copy Master Prompt and paste it into your image generator.
You can't break anything — every box is editable and nothing is saved permanently.

How the screen is laid out

Top: the black box = your finished prompt. It rewrites itself as you type. You don't type here.

Middle: the form, in numbered sections 1–7. Work top to bottom.

Bottom-right: the round button = your shot timeline (the number is your shot count).

Bottom-left: this manual button.

Very bottom: the Save & Export buttons, with a prompt-strength meter.

3 things to know first

1. Grey text is only an example. The faint grey hint in a box is not in your prompt — only what you type counts.

2. Violet menus fill boxes for you. Open a — suggest — menu, pick an option, and it drops ready-made text in. Edit it or ignore it.

3. Everything updates live. No “generate” button — the prompt at the top is always current.

Sections 1–4: the basics

Target Generation Model (top of Section 1) — pick which AI model you’ll paste the prompt into (Seedream, Nano Banana, GPT Image…). It’s written into the prompt so the render target is clear. Leave it “model-agnostic” to keep it generic.

1 · Header & Environment — project title, shot count/runtime, colour palette, and a one-line description of the world.

2 · Character Casting — name, portrait angles, appearance & attire, studio lighting, and an acting note.

3 · Location Scout — describe the places (backgrounds) as wide establishing shots.

4 · Global Presets — two menus (camera + lighting) that apply to every shot at once, so you don't set them one by one.

Section 5: shots + reference images

Each “frame” is one camera shot. Add as many as you need with + Add New Shot Frame; remove one with its red Delete button.

Reference Image. At the top of every shot is a dashed green box. Pick which uploaded picture the frame belongs to (Image 01, Image 02…) or type your own. A chip ↦ IMAGE 02 then shows the link, and it's written into the prompt.

Each shot also has: Duration (how long the shot runs, e.g. 2s, 3s), Lens (wide vs zoomed), Shot Size (wide→close-up), Camera Direction (moves and angles like POV, over-the-shoulder, low/high, Dutch), Blocking, Lighting, a one-line Action Beat, and a Transition / Cut into the next shot (hard cut, dissolve, match cut…). Every field has a suggestion menu, and the per-shot durations are totalled for you under the Shot Count box.

Section 6 & custom notes

6 · Atmosphere, Sound & Visuals — mood reference stills, audio/music tone, camera & colour science (Sony VENICE, ARRI, Panavision…), and overall visual-language keywords.

Custom Notes — press the amber + Add Custom Note to add any extra directive; they appear under a “DIRECTOR'S NOTES” strip in the prompt.

The shot timeline

  1. Tap the round button (bottom-right) to open it.
  2. Every shot is listed top-to-bottom with its reference image, lens · size, camera move, and action.
  3. Tap any item to jump straight to that shot's card — it flashes so you can spot it.

Prompt strength meter

At the bottom (and as a coloured dot at the very top) the tool shows how complete your prompt is, from red → amber → green with a percentage.

It rises as you fill in more fields, describe each shot fully, and tag reference images. The hint line tells you what to add next to strengthen it. Around 85%+ it's production-ready.

Watch the preview light up. The prompt starts dim and every word you fill in brightens — the [placeholders] stay dark. The more complete it is, the brighter it glows, so you can literally see your progress.

Saving & exporting

Copy Master Prompt — copies the whole prompt to paste into your image generator.

Save as TXT / HTML — downloads the prompt into your Downloads (or Files) folder. On a few phone browsers the file opens in a new tab instead — just use the browser’s share/save button from there. Either way the tool stays open with your work intact.

The tool doesn’t auto-save your form. Copy or download your prompt before you close or refresh the page.

Tips & FAQ

Work top to bottom. You don't have to fill everything — empty boxes just show a [bracket] placeholder in the prompt.

Unsure what to write? Open the field's violet menu and pick an example.

Lost in a long shot list? Use the timeline (bottom-right) to jump.

Nothing saved after closing? That's expected — always copy or download first.

Day / Night & the guide-glow

☀ / ☾ button (top-right). Tap it to switch between the dark Night look and a bright Day look — light background, dark text — which is far easier to read outdoors in strong sunlight. Your choice is remembered on this device.

The green guide-glow. One section header softly pulses green to show you what to do next. Sections you’ve finished stay a calm green; the ones further down are gently dimmed until it’s their turn. Fill the glowing section in and the glow moves to the next one on its own. When every section is complete, they all rest.
